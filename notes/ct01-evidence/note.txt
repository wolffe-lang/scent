Prediction
----------
Committed first as 072f663 (89c0a8f before rebase), notes/ct01-prediction.txt.

- Predicted bracket/interval constructs would dominate first disagreements:
  wrong. Repetitions of zero-width assertions, their warnings and GNU's
  distinct selection/span paths dominated the first full run.
- Predicted 92% first generated agreement: wrong. Correct per-pair recount:
  BRE 15707/20000 (78.535%), ERE 15107/20000 (75.535%). The first log initially
  counted every line of a failing group as different; raw outputs were kept
  and exact pair accounting replaced that conservative tally.
- Predicted -w retries shorter same-start matches: true for literal
  alternatives, incomplete for general repeated expressions. A bounded
  prefix-shift matrix established the reviewed span divergences instead of
  inventing a case-based matcher.
- Predicted -o prints no empty span: confirmed. Selection is separate; the
  driver calls next_span only after line_matches succeeds. A 1 MiB empty
  pattern unit protects against quadratic empty-span stepping.
- Predicted ERE/BRE backreference equivalence: ordinary rows agree, with a
  reviewed double-star extension family separated from agreement.
- Predicted (a?){n}a{n} would be hardest to keep linear: not borne out by
  measured search cost. It finds a prefix and remains effectively constant
  over the three long-line sizes; the failing (a|aa)*c family scans the line.

Cost tables (kasumi, ct01-ci, taskset 12–15, shared lock).
Each regular row is the median of three in-process match timings for
line selection plus the first nonempty span. These are per-search bounds,
not a throughput claim for an entire repeated -o iteration. Compile time
is measured separately. GNU exposes no compile-only API: empty-input
wall time includes process startup/compilation, full wall time includes
matching too. Do not subtract noisy startup times or call these speed ratios.
Engine process wall time includes <=5ms wait4 sampling granularity; native
match_ns is measured inside the driver. RSS is the exact child peak from
wait4, including startup, code, input and allocator overhead.
